## The DeGray investigation

Validated against `prf_dam.npt` — 24 measured temperature casts at the dam
through 1980, ~50 points each. Matched runs, 30 layers:

| | overall RMSE | 5–10 m | 20–30 m | 30–60 m |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| no wind stress | **2.29 °C** | −3.91 | −0.83 | −0.55 |
| Richardson + wind | 3.59 °C | −1.11 | +3.22 | +3.65 |
| k-epsilon + wind | **3.42 °C** | −1.23 | +2.95 | +3.39 |
| no mixing at all + wind | 5.87 °C | +0.84 | +5.78 | +6.21 |

Wind stress fixes the epilimnion — 3 m modelled against 14 m measured without it.
The interior bias it costs is **advective, not mixing**: removing mixing makes it
worse. And the overturning cell is inflated by a one-segment-wide grid — the same
synthetic basin transports 39% less heat below 20 m at eight cells wide.

### Defects found, each by measurement

## Caveats worth carrying

**DeGray is not a code-to-code validation.** The distribution ships inputs but no
output. It is now validated against *measurements*, which is stronger — but it is
not a comparison against W2.

**DeGray exercises no three-dimensionality.** One segment wide by construction.
Everything the engine was built for is still untested on a real basin.

**The k-epsilon closure is off by default.** A 5% improvement does not justify
changing every mixing coefficient under existing results.
