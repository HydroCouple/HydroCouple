Stateful adapters (G2.4), on the interface's new adapter members.
- RelaxationAdaptedOutput is differentiable and checkpointable; its state
  is its own previous values. Adjoint with t = ybar_out + ybar_state:
  xbar = lambda t, ybar_prev = (1 - lambda) t, lambdabar = sum t (x - y_prev).
  The token is the state. Lambda is read at every refresh, not latched.
- DifferentiableWorkflow routes cotangents per output (root or adapted) and
  reverses the adapter trees on a component's outputs, leaves first, before
  its vjp: each adapter's vjp once per step with its consumers' sum and its
  carried state. Checkpoints carry adapter tokens; a restore refuses up
  front if a stateful adapter would be left out; a stateful adapter that is
  not checkpointable is refused for multi-step backward, on or off the
  differentiated path. StateGradient gained owner/adapter; findState() takes
  any owner.
- LinearTransformAdaptedOutput reads its coefficients at every refresh, and
  TorchComposition initializes no adapter (initialize() refreshed everything
  below it and moved a stateful adapter off its rewound state).
- After a restore the engine refreshes stateless adapters, then restores
  stateful ones. Refused by name: a lagged self-coupling, a stateful adapter
  fed from outside the workflow, a seed on a non-differentiable adapter.
  Adapters fed from outside the workflow are differentiated again.
- Relaxation tokens hold each value's bit pattern and are validated.
- AbstractAdaptedOutput::states() defaults to none;
  TemporalInterpolationAdaptedOutput lists its sample record.
- Gates: 17 new differentiation tests (+2 rewritten), 2 Torch tests;
  falsifier rows S1-S20, L4; E1/E4/E5/E8/T11 rewritten.

ABI-4 enforcement (2026-10-01):

ABI 4 let items and components declare what their numbers mean and which
status transitions are lawful; nothing in the SDK read the declarations.

- setStatus() on AbstractModelComponent and AbstractWorkflowComponent checks
  each transition against the normative tables. An illegal one is performed
  (refusing Done would hang an orchestrator) and reported on errors() as an
  Error with Diagnostic::IllegalStatusTransition (1001), once per (from, to)
  pair per object. Status is held in a std::atomic. Codes are named in the
  new core/diagnosticcodes.h.
- validateConnection(provider, consumer) compares the two ends of a link:
  element type, value definition (quantity/quality, categories, ValueKind,
  unit dimension and scale), TimeKind and interval, interpolation and
  extrapolation policy, horizontal and vertical reference systems, mesh
  location, vector structure and basis, layering, dimension roles. Error
  when both ends declare and contradict, Warning when one is silent.
  AbstractWorkflowComponent::validate() runs it over every wired link via
  the new virtual validateConnections(); Errors fail the workflow.
- ComponentWorker stamps its component's status into every reply
  (kFlagStatusPresent | status); ProxyModelComponent mirrors it, so a remote
  that reaches Done is seen as Done, and walks the lawful intermediate
  states. A rejected validate leaves the proxy Invalid, not Failed.
- TimeSeriesOutput/TimeSeriesInput declare TimeInterpolation::None and
  TimeExtrapolation::Refuse, which is what they do.
