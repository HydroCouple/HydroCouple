## 2. The shape of it: navigation is arithmetic now

`scene/navigation.{h,cpp}` is new and is the point of the phase. Every
decision the 3D view's navigation can get *wrong* — which way a drag
turns the camera, which way a wheel notch goes and whether inverting it
is exactly reciprocal, whether a press pans, where "reset" resets to —
is a free function over plain values, gated and falsified off-machine.

That is the U4 lesson applied again. These used to live inside
`QRhiWidget` event handlers where no gate under the offscreen platform
could reach them (D31), and they are precisely the bugs a user feels in
the first five seconds and no existing test could see.

`SceneView` now reads a preference and applies a result. It holds no
navigation arithmetic of its own.

## 3. Deliberate choices worth your eye

**The menu dispatches; the ribbon does not.** *Zoom In / Out / Full
Extent* in the View menu keep their existing behaviour — they act on
whichever view is in front, so a shortcut always means "zoom what I see".
The ribbon's 3D Navigate group acts on the scene **unconditionally**,
because a button on the 3D strip that moved the map (because the map
happened to be in front) would be a button that did nothing visible. Like
the exaggeration spin already on that strip, each brings the 3D view
forward first. This is the plan's own reasoning: "means the same thing in
each" holds for a shortcut and not for a place.

**`pressShouldPan` is not "else if" by accident.** A shift-left press
that pans must not also start an orbit or a rubber band, or one drag does
two things. The orbit and band flags are now explicitly `!m_panning &&
…`.

**Middle and right always pan, under every setting.** The preference only
decides whether shift-with-left pans *as well*. That exists for trackpads
and mice with no middle button, where otherwise there is no way to pan at
all. Taking the old convention away would break muscle memory to no
purpose — mutation N12 restores that bug.

**"Look at Selection" with nothing selected says so** rather than framing
everything. Leaping to the whole model is the kind of helpfulness that
loses someone the position they spent a minute finding. It posts to the
status bar instead. Same for both *Sync now* faces when the source view
has nothing to hand over.

**The selection's extent goes through `expandTo`, not `QRectF::united`.**
Fifth occurrence of the same trap (D26): `united()` discards a rectangle
it calls null, a zero-area one is null, and a selection of point features
would have framed the last one and lost the rest.

**Top View and the gizmo's Up arm are gated against each other.** Two
controls that both mean "look down" must not take the user to two
different places; `TopViewAndTheGizmosUpArmMeanTheSameThing` is that gate.
Both leave the azimuth alone, because a plan view has no meaningful one.

## 4. One thing the falsifier deleted

`wheelDollyFactor` had an early return for a wheel event reporting no
movement. Mutation N10 removed it and every gate stayed green — because
`pow(x, 0)` is *exactly* 1.0 for any finite x, so the branch could not
change an answer. It is gone. The gate that checks the promise stays, and
a new one pins the contract that actually is load-bearing: **one notch
moves by exactly the notch size.** Qt reports eighths of a degree and a
notch is fifteen of them, so dividing by 8 instead of 120 would leave
every other property intact and make the wheel fifteen times too
sensitive — that is N10 now, and it bites.

## 6. Look at it

With a model loaded:

1. **3D tab ▸ Navigate.** The group is new. *Zoom In* and *Zoom Out* move
   the scene by one wheel notch each. *Full Extent* frames everything.
2. **From the Map tab, press a 3D Navigate button.** It switches to 3D
   *and* acts. That is the rule for everything on that strip.
3. **Reset View** from any angle: north up, tilted 45°, exactly where a
   new view starts. **Top View**: straight down, and the map must **not**
   spin — whatever azimuth you were at, you keep.
4. **Select something in the map, switch to 3D, press Look at Selection.**
   It frames that. Press it with nothing selected: a status-bar message,
   and the view does not move.
5. **Preferences ▸ 3D View ▸ Link 2D and 3D views → Never.** Frame the map
   somewhere, switch to 3D: the 3D view keeps its own framing. Switch
   back: the map keeps its own. Set it back to *On tab switch* and confirm
   the old behaviour returns.
6. **Sync from Map** (3D strip) and **Sync from 3D** (Map strip) do the
   hand-off on demand, under either setting.
7. **Orbit sensitivity** to 0.05, then to 1.2 — the drag gets noticeably
   slower and faster. The spin box should refuse to go outside that range.
8. **Invert the 3D scroll wheel.** The wheel reverses, and by exactly the
   same amount in each direction.
9. **Pan the 3D view with → Middle, right, or Shift and left drag.**
   Shift-left now pans. Check that it does **not** also orbit or draw a
   band, and that with the setting back on *Middle or right*, shift-left
   goes back to adding to the selection (U6).
