# Authors

HydroCouple is the component-based modeling interface standard described in Buahin &
Horsburgh (2018), developed and maintained by the contributors listed below.

## Project Lead

- **Caleb Buahin** [@cbuahin](https://github.com/cbuahin) ([ORCID 0000-0002-9859-2264](https://orcid.org/0000-0002-9859-2264)) — Author and maintainer of the HydroCouple interface standard: the C++ interface headers (component lifecycle, exchange items, argument and value definitions, geospatial and temporal data types, and the HPC interfaces), the Python bindings, the test suite, the CMake/vcpkg packaging, and the documentation.

## Contributors

No contributions from outside the project lead have been merged to date. Contributors
whose work is incorporated into HydroCouple will be recognized here with their name,
affiliation (if any), and the scope of their contribution, per
[CONTRIBUTING.md §5](./CONTRIBUTING.md#5-author-acknowledgment).

If you believe your contribution has been omitted or mis-described, please open an issue
or contact the Technical Manager directly.

## Foundational Publication

The interface standard originates in the following peer-reviewed work. Co-authorship of
that publication is recorded here for attribution; it does not by itself denote
contribution to this repository's source.

- **Caleb A. Buahin** and **Jeffery S. Horsburgh** ([ORCID 0000-0002-0768-3196](https://orcid.org/0000-0002-0768-3196)) — *Advancing the Open Modeling Interface (OpenMI) for integrated water resources modeling.* Environmental Modelling & Software 108, 133–153 (2018). [doi:10.1016/j.envsoft.2018.07.015](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.envsoft.2018.07.015)
